# 03 — Competitor Analysis & SERP Breakdown

*Milestone I, item 3 · Crawl-Smart Catalogue · October 2026*

Method: manual SERP inspection (Google India, buy-intent queries per category,
October 2026). Rankings shift daily — this is a snapshot, not a rank tracker.

## Cross-category findings

- **Marketplaces own head terms.** Amazon.in listings (Xacton, TIPAKKA,
  KHOOBREZ cable boxes at ₹489–₹899; 1,500+ ratings on leaders) and IKEA India
  (SÄTTING cable box ₹599; SINNERLIG bamboo lamp ₹5,980) rank top-5 for every
  head term inspected, with Shopping carousels, star ratings and image packs.
- **Eco-D2C niche stores own the modifiers.** Bamboostan, Ecolo Store,
  Silpakarman (bamboo); Scrapshala/Suspire, Minus Degre, Everyday Organisers
  (upcycled/recycled organizers); Ninecork, Tjori, Upciclo (cork);
  Timalfi, GreenVayu, Bamboo Eco-Hub (bamboo lighting). Typical range
  ₹399–₹2,999 — our demo prices (₹499–₹1,899) sit inside the real band.
- **B2B/gifting pages pollute informational intent.** IndiaMART and corporate
  gifting sites (MOQ 100 pcs) rank for "recycled desk organiser" and
  "cork desk mat" — weak content our guides can out-teach.
- **Content gap (our opening):** nobody owns *[material] + [product] + hostel /
  small-desk* long-tails with genuinely helpful content. Competitor PDPs are
  thin on specs (missing dimensions/compatibility tables our product pages
  carry) and none structure buying guides with step/comparison schema content.

## Takeaway for strategy

Do not contest head terms against Amazon/IKEA. Win position 1–3 on
long-tail material×use-case queries (doc 02), then use internal linking
(guides → categories → products) to consolidate topical authority so category
pages can climb toward head terms over time.
